01 · DECODE THE MEASUREMENT

Dispersion measure is an electron count along a line—not a distance meter.

Cold plasma delays low radio frequencies by an amount proportional to DM × ν⁻². The curve is a strong astrophysical signature. But total DM combines our Galaxy, its halo, intergalactic gas, the host galaxy and the immediate burst environment.
